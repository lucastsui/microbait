A reading desk, not a feed

The internet can talk. You do not have to hear the bait.

Write what you actually want to know. Microbait looks through public posts and news, drops the ads and the shock, and hands back a short briefing in a neutral voice.

How a briefing is made

1. You name a goal

A sentence is enough. Recent tech. Whether named public people posted. What changed in a field you already follow.

2. The desk reads in public

With a SpaceXAI key, Grok searches the web and X. Without one, Microbait still reads public RSS and Hacker News.

3. The voice is flattened

Ads, codes, panic words, and like-counts are stripped. What remains is who, what, when, and where, plus the source.

What this will not pretend to do

Private friends

Facebook stories, locked Instagram, and DMs stay closed unless those companies later grant a login. Public X usernames can be listed today.

No original feed

You never see the timeline. That is the point. Promoted posts do not get a slot on the page.

Not a siren

There are no badges, streaks, or breaking bars. If nothing new matches the goal, the desk says so and stops.
